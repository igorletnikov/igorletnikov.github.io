shorts-factory

This private tool helps produce English educational Shorts, uploads videos its owner has approved to the owner's own YouTube channels, manages their publication schedule, and reads those channels' analytics and comments to improve future Shorts. It also searches public YouTube videos for format and style research. Uploads and channel changes are limited to the owner's own channels.
